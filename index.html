Music commands

Eight commands cover the whole party. Select any command to copy it.

  • !playPlay a song from YouTube, Spotify, or URL.
  • !joinConnect the bot to your voice channel without playing music.
  • !skipSkip the currently playing song.
  • !queueView the current music queue.
  • !247Toggle 24/7 mode in the voice channel.
  • !setthemeSet your personal entrance theme song.
  • !panelSpawn the interactive UI player control panel.
  • !loadLoad and play your saved personal playlists.
