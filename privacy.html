Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Bobo Bot in your server, we may collect and store the following information to ensure the Bot functions correctly:

  • Server IDs and Channel IDs (for configuration and settings)
  • User IDs (only when using specific commands or saving playlists)
  • Message content is NOT stored permanently unless explicitly saved (e.g., in a bug report).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for:

  • Providing the Bot's core functionality (e.g., Music Queues, Playlists, Auto-Role, 24/7 mode).
  • Improving the Bot's performance and squashing bugs.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. Your data is kept secure and private on our servers.

4. Data Deletion

If you remove Bobo Bot from your server, you can request the deletion of your server's data by contacting our support team or the Bot Developer directly.

5. Message Handling

The Bot reads messages in your server in order to respond to commands and operate its features. As part of its functionality, the Bot may automatically delete messages (for example, to keep channels tidy). Message content that is deleted is not stored.
